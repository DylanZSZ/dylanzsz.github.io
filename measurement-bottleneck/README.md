# Measurement Is the Bottleneck of Auto-Research

A standalone interactive position piece based on two months of automated post-training research: 25 campaigns, 283 search lanes, and 8,298 trained candidates.

The article argues that autoresearch becomes viable only when a problem has an evaluator that is cheap, repeatable, and demonstrably faithful to the real target. It includes a readiness matrix, a seven-gate evaluator test, and examples of problems that are ready, conditional, or not yet suitable for a closed loop.
